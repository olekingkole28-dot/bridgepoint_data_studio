# BridgePoint Autonomous Data Machine V548

## Purpose

BridgePoint operates as a guarded self-improving property-intelligence network rather than a collection of disconnected jobs. The V548 autonomy governor measures demand, verified data supply, source health, geographic readiness, customer outcomes and commercial truth, then allocates internal attention to the largest information gaps.

## Control loop

1. **Observe** — read source health, national expansion depth, acquisition sessions, paid/trial truth, estimate runs, benchmark eligibility, customer outcomes and IoT observations.
2. **Price information gaps** — create an internal market book. `imbalance_score` is demand divided by verified supply; it is not a financial/security price.
3. **Prioritize** — rank intelligence markets, states/DC and unhealthy/stale sources.
4. **Act internally** — preserve automatic BridgePoint-owned data health and priority allocation while existing source/expansion/model/product workers perform their specialized jobs.
5. **Gate consequential actions** — real estimate ingestion, sensor connections and other customer/external evidence acquisition remain approval/authorization dependent.
6. **Learn** — verified outcomes feed the existing source-value optimizer and model-promotion gates; no learning claim is made before sufficient outcomes exist.
7. **Repeat** — `bridgepoint-autonomous-data-machine-v548` runs at minute 06/21/36/51 each hour.

## Internal information markets

- `CLAIM_EVIDENCE` — trial/audit demand vs real estimate runs and benchmark-eligible claims.
- `NATIONAL_PROPERTY_DEPTH` — national coverage demand vs states/DC with actual opportunity depth.
- `WEATHER_INTELLIGENCE` — weather-tool/storm-ledger demand vs available weather/peril signal supply.
- `LIVE_PROPERTY_STATE` — property-health/digital demand vs authorized IoT observations.
- `DEVELOPER_DISTRIBUTION` — developer interest vs published open tooling/API supply.

The market maker allocates **data collection, validation, productization and distribution attention**. It does not autonomously speculate on, buy, sell or finance property.

## Hard policy defaults

Enabled:
- autonomy governor
- automatic internal data actions
- automatic priority allocation

Disabled by default:
- automatic public publication
- external messaging
- external spend
- payment execution
- property transactions
- insurance submissions
- physical dispatch
- security bypass/exploitation

These are stored in `bridgepoint_ai.autonomy_policy_v548` and are visible in the owner Autonomy Center.

## First validated cycle

V548 completed its first clean cycle after schema-level fixes. At that moment, national property depth ranked as the largest information imbalance, claim/estimate evidence remained a major shortage, live-state telemetry had zero real IoT observations, ARR remained zero, and one qualified commercial trial was recognized by the commercial-truth filters.

## Design principle

BridgePoint should become increasingly self-operating by automating repetitive observation, prioritization, data processing, validation routing and product feedback. Consequential actions remain explicitly authorized. The goal is a self-sustaining data machine, not an unbounded autonomous agent.
