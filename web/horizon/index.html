Questions, answered

Am I claiming legal ownership of a house?

No. A Horizon home-base reservation is a game identity anchored to a requested location. It does not change title, ownership, access rights, or any real-world legal interest.

Will Horizon work on a phone?

Phone play is a design target alongside desktop and controller input. The world requires aggressive level-of-detail streaming and scalable graphics, so device support expands through playtesting rather than being assumed.

Do I have to invite friends?

No. Referrals are optional. Successful referrals can unlock digital founding rewards and increase faction momentum.

What happens before the full game is finished?

Founding access begins with account/home-base reservations, factions, world previews and increasingly playable systems. Full release follows validated playable builds.

What makes the map different?

Horizon is designed around BridgePoint's property/spatial truth layer rather than a small handcrafted fictional map. Visual fidelity and playable detail will increase in stages as the world pipeline matures.
