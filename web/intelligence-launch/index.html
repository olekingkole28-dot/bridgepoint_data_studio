Questions, answered

What is a canonical property?

A reconciled BridgePoint identity for a real property after normalization, source matching and deduplication. It anchors evidence from many sources to the same place.

Why are there fewer addresses than properties?

Not every property has a situs or mailing address, and multi-unit or special land identities can have different address relationships. BridgePoint tracks property and address identity separately.

What does Founding Member access give me?
