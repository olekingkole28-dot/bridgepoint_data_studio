## Public-record and copyright basis for the narrow projection

Georgia Code § 50-18-70 states a strong public policy favoring access to government records and defines public records to include computer-based/generated information, data, and data fields prepared or maintained by an agency.

- `https://law.justia.com/codes/georgia/title-50/chapter-18/article-4/section-50-18-70/`

The U.S. Copyright Office explains that facts/data points themselves are not protected by copyright, while sufficiently creative selection/coordination/arrangement of a database may be protected. BridgePoint therefore extracts only factual parcel identifiers under this candidate and does not reproduce Cobb's database arrangement or paid GIS product.

- `https://www.copyright.gov/non-photographic-databases/register-one.html`
- `https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-general.html`
