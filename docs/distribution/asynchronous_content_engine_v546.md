# BridgePoint Asynchronous Content Engine V546

Goal: let useful technical/property/claims content educate the market without founder demos or live sales calls.

## Format ladder

One source topic should be reusable as:
1. short written post;
2. 60–180 second audio/video explanation;
3. technical GitHub note or utility;
4. longer audio/podcast episode if/when BridgePoint operates a show;
5. CTA to the free utility, self-guided tour, or app.

## First four topics

### 1. What an NWS warning can — and cannot — prove about a property
Hook: Weather alerts are valuable evidence context, but they are not property-level damage proof.
Free utility CTA: NWS Claim Weather Formatter.

### 2. Estimate variance is not automatically insurance underpayment
Hook: Missing scope, quantity differences and price variance are different problems and should be measured separately.
Research CTA: National Property Estimate Variance Index.

### 3. Why property databases need a live-state layer
Hook: parcel/permit/history data explains the past; weather, sensors, verified field evidence and updated twins can describe changing present conditions.
Enterprise CTA: BridgePoint enterprise page.

### 4. What BridgePoint means by transparent AI readiness
Hook: every capability should expose what is live, shadow/testing, data-required, integration-required, or building—and what it is prohibited from claiming.
Enterprise CTA: public capabilities manifest.

## Truth rules

Never manufacture:
- customer count;
- recovered/supplement dollars;
- carrier wrongdoing;
- property damage;
- actuarial accuracy;
- partner/integration status.

When a capability is in SHADOW, DATA_REQUIRED, CREDENTIALS_REQUIRED, or PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED state, content must say so when material to the claim.

## If a podcast/show is launched

A possible recurring structure:
- 30 sec: real industry/data problem;
- 3–8 min: explain the data/mechanics;
- 1–3 min: what BridgePoint is testing/building;
- 30 sec: one useful free link;
- show notes include methodology/limitations.

Do not create a podcast solely to create another marketing channel. Use it only if the same research already being produced for BridgePoint can feed it with low incremental effort.
