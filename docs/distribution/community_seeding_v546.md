# BridgePoint Community Seeding Playbook V546

Purpose: earn inbound trials from text-based contractor/adjuster/developer communities without mass spam, fabricated results, or unsupported insurance claims.

## Core rule

Lead with a free utility, methodology, or useful answer. Link BridgePoint only where community rules permit promotion or where a user explicitly asks for the tool.

Do not claim that BridgePoint "finds underpaid claims" as a universal fact. Preferred language:

> BridgePoint compares authorized estimate line items with evidence-supported repair scope and flags missing scope, quantity differences, and price variance for human review.

## Zero-pressure message

Hey — I built a free NWS weather formatter for claim/property work. You give it a coordinate and it turns NWS alert records into copy-ready timeline notes. No account required. If it is useful, the full BridgePoint app goes further into property evidence and estimate comparison. Free tool: https://bridgepointintelligence.online/tools/nws-claim-weather-formatter/

## Smart Supplement tester message

Hey — I'm looking for a few claim professionals willing to test a new estimate-comparison workflow on a de-identified/authorized estimate. BridgePoint normalizes estimate lines and flags missing scope, quantity differences, and price variance for human review. No promise that a variance is coverage owed or guaranteed payment. Self-guided tour: https://bridgepointintelligence.online/start/

## Developer/data-community message

I open-sourced a small zero-dependency Python utility that formats NWS alert data for a point into claim-note text. The broader project is a property-intelligence platform connecting property identity, weather, evidence, timelines and estimate variance. Feedback on the utility/API assumptions is welcome.

## Where to participate

- roofing / restoration communities where tool sharing is permitted
- public-adjusting / claims communities where non-solicitation rules permit it
- insurance-technology and geospatial developer communities
- contractor software / automation Discord or Slack workspaces
- GitHub discussions/issues where the tool directly solves the topic being discussed

Always inspect the current rules before posting. Do not evade subreddit/community promotion restrictions by disguising an ad as neutral advice.

## Stop conditions

Do not post when:
- the community explicitly bans self-promotion or vendor links;
- the message would require inventing a customer result;
- the only hook is "carrier underpayment" without verified evidence;
- personal/private claim information would be exposed;
- the same copy has already been posted repeatedly in the same community.

## Measurement

Use UTM-tagged URLs and BridgePoint acquisition tracking to measure:
- utility visits;
- tour starts;
- app registrations;
- trial activation;
- first meaningful workflow;
- eventual paid conversion.

The goal is not post volume. The goal is qualified inbound behavior.
